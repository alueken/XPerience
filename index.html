STEM CAMPS

Curriculum Development & Mentorship

Design, build, and prototype learning kits for three distinct youth STEM camps, including curriculum for upcoming future camps.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Prototyping physical electronics or science demo kits
  • Drafting lesson plans & instructor slide-decks
  • Camp logistics & leading interactive student cohorts
BUSINESS CONSULTING

Client Consulting & Real-World Solutions

Collaborate directly with regional businesses to assess bottlenecks, draft engineering proposals, and build technology answers.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Client discovery calls & drafting statements of work
  • Developing custom software dashboards or hardware prototypes
  • Final presentation decks & pitch delivery to executives
OPENBRAILLE

Assistive Technology R&D

Expand on XPerience's core project: OpenBraille. Designing open-source, affordable tactile displays, microcode, and tooling.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Mechanical modeling in CAD & 3D printing optimization
  • Embedded software (C++/Arduino) for solenoid/actuator control
  • Interactive accessibility testing & user documentation
ORG GROWTH

Organizational Growth & Outreach

Help scale XPerience. Design marketing content, pitch non-profit partners, run social campaigns, and help draft key grants.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Creating compelling video recaps of STEM Camps
  • Researching local educational foundations & writing drafts
  • Formulating community engagement & recruitment events

Review manager feedback on your logs and pull requests. Implement changes as we grow!
